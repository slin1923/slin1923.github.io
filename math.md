# Math Notes

## Linear Algebra

### Eigenvalues

The eigenvalue equation:

$$
A v = \lambda v
$$

Interpretation:
- $v$ = invariant direction
- $\lambda$ = scaling factor

## Optimization

### Gradient Descent

$$
x_{k+1} = x_k - \alpha \nabla f(x_k)
$$

Key idea:
- Move opposite the gradient
- Step size $\alpha$ controls stability
